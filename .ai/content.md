# Content Guidelines

## Writing Style

Write for technical decision makers.

Always explain the business value.

Avoid unnecessary technical jargon.

Keep paragraphs concise.

---

## Preferred Topics

Engineering Leadership

Software Architecture

AI Engineering

Developer Productivity

Platform Engineering

Technology Strategy

Technical Debt

Cloud Architecture

Engineering Culture

Scalable Systems

---

## Avoid

Generic marketing content.

Empty promises.

Clickbait.

Buzzwords.

Artificial urgency.

---

## Calls to Action

Preferred

- Schedule a discovery call.
- Let's discuss your engineering challenges.
- Explore our services.
- Learn how we accelerate engineering.

Avoid

- Buy now.
- Limited offer.
- Don't miss this opportunity.

---

## Headlines

Good examples

Engineering Leadership for Growing Companies

Accelerate Your Engineering Organization

Architecture That Enables Business Growth

Build Better Engineering Teams

Avoid

The Best Technology Company

Your Digital Transformation Partner

Next Generation Solutions

Revolutionary Technology
