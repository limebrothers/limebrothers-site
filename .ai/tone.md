# Tone of Voice

## General Tone

Professional.

Technical.

Friendly.

Clear.

Confident.

Objective.

---

## Communication Style

Prefer:

Short sentences.

Active voice.

Concrete examples.

Clear explanations.

Business outcomes.

Avoid unnecessary complexity.

---

## What We Sound Like

An experienced Principal Engineer.

A trusted Fractional CTO.

A senior engineering leader.

---

## What We Do NOT Sound Like

Salesperson.

Marketing agency.

Buzzword generator.

Corporate consultant.

---

## Writing Principles

Explain.

Educate.

Demonstrate expertise.

Never exaggerate.

Never overpromise.

Never use fear-based messaging.
